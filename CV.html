Summary & Research Interests

Ab initio studies of electronic and magnetic properties of strongly correlated rare-earth and transition-metal based quantum materials, with focus on 4f crystal-field splitting, magnetism, spin-phonon coupling, magnonic excitations, and quantum defects for quantum information applications.

  • Crystal-field splitting of rare-earth ions
  • Rare-earth and transition-metal magnetism
  • Quantum defects
  • Magnonic excitations
  • Spin-phonon coupling
